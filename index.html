百姓Hyakushō

How to hold a village together

Four peasants, one valley, and three years. Nothing happens until you end the turn, so there is no hurry — only consequences.

  1. The levy is the whole game. After each harvest the daimyō's collectors come for rice: 40 koku, then 75, then 115. Miss two and the village is broken up. The countdown is in the bar at the top.
  2. Rice comes from paddies, and only from paddies. Foraging and hunting will not cover a levy. Tap Build → Rice Paddy early and often.
  3. Build shelter before winter. A Minka sleeps two; villagers without a bed lose heart. A Hearth Fire keeps anyone within five tiles alive through the cold. A Kura raises how much you can store — everything over the cap rots.
  4. You give orders, not commands. Choose a work order or a building, then drag across the map. Villagers pick up the nearest job they are willing to do; the toggles under each name in Village decide what that is.
  5. Bandits come from year two. Villagers fight automatically when one gets close. A Yagura makes them fight harder; an Ishigaki makes bandits waste turns breaking through.

Moving the map: drag with no tool chosen to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a tile to inspect it, tap a villager to select them. While a tool is chosen, dragging paints instead — tap the chip at the bottom of the map to put it down.

Keyboard: Space end turn · B build menu · C/M/F work orders · X cancel tool · Esc put the tool down · H this page
