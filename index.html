Hearthfall

How Hearthfall works

Four settlers, one valley, and three years to survive. Nothing happens until you end the turn — you have all the time you want to plan.

  • Give orders, not commands. Paint work orders on the map (Chop, Mine, Forage) and drop blueprints (1–5). Drag to cover an area. Colonists pick up the nearest job they are willing to do.
  • Everyone has a mind of their own. Each colonist has skills, hunger, tiredness and a mood. Use the toggles beside their name to decide which kinds of work they will accept.
  • Eat, sleep, repeat. Food is a shared stockpile. Rest needs a bed, and a bed needs a house — sleeping rough wrecks morale.
  • Winter is the test. Crops stop growing and anyone more than five tiles from a campfire loses health. Stockpile in autumn.
  • Raiders come from year two. Colonists fight automatically when raiders get close. Stone walls buy you time.
