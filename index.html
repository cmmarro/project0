Hearthfall

How Hearthfall works

Four settlers, one valley, and three years to survive. Nothing happens until you end the turn — you have all the time you want to plan.

  • Give orders, not commands. Pick a tool from the bar at the bottom, then drag across the map to mark work or drop blueprints. Colonists pick up the nearest job they are willing to do.
  • Everyone has a mind of their own. Each colonist has skills, hunger, tiredness and a mood. Open Colony to see them and to choose which kinds of work each one will accept.
  • Eat, sleep, repeat. Food is a shared stockpile. Rest needs a bed, and a bed needs a house — sleeping rough wrecks morale.
  • Winter is the test. Crops stop growing and anyone more than five tiles from a campfire loses health. Stockpile in autumn.
  • Raiders come from year two. Colonists fight automatically when raiders get close. Stone walls buy you time.

Moving the map: drag with no tool selected to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a tile to inspect it, tap a colonist to select them.

Keyboard: Space end turn · C/M/F orders · 1–5 buildings · X cancel tool · Esc deselect · H this sheet
