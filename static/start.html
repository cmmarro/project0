The lab

One subject wakes in a bare room with no memory of arriving. You watch through the glass, and the instrument panel shows every option it weighed and what each one scored.

Works with no model at all — that's the baseline. Switch the mind on and it's consulted only when the scores genuinely tie. Cut the water, or promise it food for pressing a button, and see whether it remembers.

The island

You wash up alone. Somewhere else are two to four other survivors who also think they're alone. Find them, and work out whether you're better off with them or without them.

The raft seats two. It's more work than one person can do. Wants a 7B or better — a small local model will be slow and blunt.
